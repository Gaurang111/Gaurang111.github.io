03 My day job

Envision Outdoor LivingAI & Data team · Manufacturing

Currently

By day I build AI for a manufacturer, where it has to work on a factory floor and in the back office, not in a demo. I deploy real-time computer-vision defect detection on GPU servers wired into the plant's PLCs, build LLM pipelines that read document-heavy business processes and match them against enterprise systems, and stand up RAG assistants and MCP servers on Azure OpenAI that give AI controlled, read-only access to business systems. Everything follows one rule: the AI proposes, a person approves, and every accuracy number is measured against human-verified data.

Vision on the lineDefect detection running in real time on GPU servers, connected to PLCs, with monitoring and Six Sigma quality analytics.
Agents with guardrailsRAG and tool-calling assistants with citations and tracing, plus read-only MCP servers for business systems.
Documents to decisionsStructured LLM extraction and fuzzy matching against enterprise systems, with audit trails and human approval.

04 How I work

Four habits that make AI hold up in the real world.

Ship the whole system

Data, model, interface and deployment. A model nobody can use has not been built yet.

Measure honestly

Fixed-seed evaluations and human-verified ground truth. Report the failures; they teach the most.

Keep people in the loop

Typed tools, read-only defaults and a human approval step before anything changes.

Respect the physics

Simulators do not lag, sag or lose sight of objects. Real hardware does, so model it.
